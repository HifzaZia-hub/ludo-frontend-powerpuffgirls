# Changelog

- P0: project scaffold with Vite, React, TypeScript, Tailwind, tooling and dark and light theme tokens (NFR-SYS-13).
- P1: contract types, board and player config, default rules and tested geometry functions (NFR-SYS-11).
- P2: standalone mock LGS with REST, sockets, game rules, computer players and debug scenarios (enables NFR-SYS-12).
- P3: `GameAdapter` with `LgsAdapter` and `FakeAdapter`, game and settings stores, friendly error copy and a temporary debug line (NFR-SYS-12, NFR-SYS-04, NFR-SYS-14, FR-SYS-04).
- P4: 17 shared components with a dev-only kitchen sink, message and rules copy, and colour tokens that meet the contrast target (NFR-SYS-07, NFR-SYS-08, NFR-SYS-01).
- P5: main screen, setup, join, lobby, settings and rules, with a stand-in game screen until P6 (FR-SYS-01, FR-SYS-10, D2 FR-07, FR-SYS-07).
- P6: read-only game screen with board, pieces, status panel, message area, crash screen and dev-only fixtures (FR-SYS-09, FR-SYS-06, NFR-SYS-10, NFR-SYS-11, NFR-SYS-03, D2 FR-07).
- P7: rolling the die, turn text, sixes counter and countdown, with the `R` key and a lint rule against `Math.random` (FR-SYS-02, NFR-SYS-05, FR-SYS-07).
- P8: choosing and moving a piece by mouse or keyboard, a destination and capture preview, step-by-step walking and Skip (FR-SYS-03, FR-SYS-04, FR-SYS-05, D2 FR-18, D2 FR-38).
- P9: event feedback in order, with the message log, capture and finish effects, banners and placeholder sounds (FR-SYS-05, FR-SYS-10, D2 FR-23, D2 FR-36, D2 FR-37).
- P10: win screen with ranking, session score and dice check, restart and leave behind confirm dialogs, and the stopped screen (FR-SYS-06, FR-SYS-08, D2 FR-29, D2 FR-33, D2 FR-34).
- P11: surviving a refresh, a lost connection and a second tab, with a reconnecting banner, error boundaries on every screen and new mock debug routes (NFR-SYS-03, NFR-SYS-04, FR-SYS-04).
- P12: the requirements pass, with `npm run e2e` for rerunnable browser checks, a dev-only log of computer turn times and a dev-only broken fixture, plus evidence for NFR-SYS-01 to NFR-SYS-10 (MVP). The script then learned to drive Firefox over WebDriver BiDi, for the Firefox row of NFR-SYS-09.
- After P12: colour choice. The host picks any colour, and a friend picks from the ones still free. The contract gains `colour` on create and join and a `COLOUR_TAKEN` error, and the room preview is now the room's snapshot (FR-SYS-01, D2 FR-06). Same-device play (P13) was dropped, because the client doesn't need it.
- After P12: the contract is a finished spec. Its 22 open questions to the backend team are decided and written into the sections they belong to (acks, versions, event data, field formats, turn order). In each one the mock already behaved that way, so no code changed. Only the date of the first connection is still open.
- After P12: GitHub runs our checks on every PR and on `main` (typecheck, lint, build, tests and the browser smoke test in Chrome), so a broken change shows up before it is merged (NFR-SYS-13).
- After P12: "Your turn — now press Move" once a piece is picked, from an expert walkthrough of a first turn, plus the D3 v1.1 change list and a demo script (NFR-SYS-06).
- P14: polish, not graded. Three more themes (Neon, Forest, Minimal), the final places revealed in turn with a Copy result button, and an optional 3D die that downloads only when turned on. 3D pieces and music were left out.
- After P14: a visual refresh. Glossy pawns whose white badge keeps each player's shape, a 3D-looking die drawn in CSS that shows only the server's value, yards with starting spots, gold safe stars and a home badge, glowing panels on a deep backdrop, and a redesigned win screen with the winner's pawn and a podium (FR-SYS-06, FR-SYS-10, NFR-SYS-05, NFR-SYS-07).
- After P14: the emerald look, from the team's mockups. The Dark theme turns green and shows the background picture; the front page gets a big 3D die you can drag or spin, the LUDO title and a feature strip; New Game becomes one card with icons; and the game's die becomes a real cube with pips on every face that settles with the server's value toward the player. It also fixes the CSS die from the visual refresh, whose side faces Tailwind had dropped (FR-SYS-01, FR-SYS-02, FR-SYS-10, NFR-SYS-05, NFR-SYS-08).
- After P14: motion and new sounds. The front page comes in piece by piece: the die drops in, the letters of LUDO rise one at a time, the pawns hop when pointed at, lights drift, and the background and die lean with the pointer. Buttons lift and glow under the pointer, primary buttons catch a sweep of light, cards light up where the pointer is, the choice highlight glides between options, and screens and dialogs ease in. In the game, the player whose turn it is glows, the pieces that can move bob, a chosen piece lifts, a walking piece hops on every square, a captured piece spins home with a burst, a piece reaching home throws sparks, the die squashes when pressed and flashes when it lands, counts flip, and new messages slide in. The win screen's crown drops on and the podium rises. All of it moves only transform and opacity, follows Animations Fast, and stops completely with Animations Off or the device's reduced-motion setting, the endless loops included. The placeholder tones become real sounds made by `scripts/make-sounds.py`: a dice rattle, a wooden tap, a swoosh and thud, chimes, a fanfare and a soft "no" (FR-SYS-05, FR-SYS-10, NFR-SYS-01).
